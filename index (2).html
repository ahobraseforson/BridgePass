FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BridgePass.

Do I need internet to use BridgePass?
+
No! BridgePass is offline-first. Download the lesson pack once, and everything works offline after that — lessons, practice questions, even the language switcher. Progress syncs when you reconnect.
Is it really free for students?
+
Yes. The core learning experience — diagnostics, micro-lessons, and progress tracking — is free for all JHS graduates in Ghana. We're exploring partnerships with GES and NGOs to keep it that way.
Which languages are supported?
+
English, Twi, Ga, and Ewe at launch. We're working with local educators to add more languages based on demand. Every lesson can be switched with one tap — no need to restart.
How does the Guardian Link work?
+
Students generate a 6-character invite code. Parents or teachers enter the code plus the student's name to link accounts. Once linked, guardians see readiness scores, weak areas, and weekly activity.
Is the content aligned with the GES curriculum?
+
Yes. All content is mapped to the Ghana Education Service JHS and SHS syllabi for Mathematics, English, and Science. We worked with GES-approved educators to ensure every concept matches national standards.
What if I don't have a smartphone?
+
We're building an SMS fallback feature. Students without smartphones can receive daily micro-lessons and progress updates via SMS. The full experience requires a smartphone or tablet, but we're committed to inclusivity.
